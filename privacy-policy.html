Privacy Policy – PSS Ledger

Effective: October 15, 2026
Operator: 1000316242 Ontario Inc., operating as Be Present Media ("we", "us"), Ontario, Canada
Contact: brad@bepresentmedia.ca

1. What PSS Ledger is

PSS Ledger is a private, internal bookkeeping application. It is used only by Be Present Media (and companies it keeps books for under a written agreement) to organise receipts and invoices and to prepare bookkeeping entries in QuickBooks Online. It is not offered to the public and is not sold or listed in any app store.

2. Information we access

  • QuickBooks Online data of the connected company, with the company owner's permission: the chart of accounts, vendors, customers, products and services, tax codes and transactions (for example expenses, bills and journal entries), and company settings.
  • Source documents such as receipts, invoices and bank or card statements, provided by the company.
  • Email, only where the company owner connects a mailbox, and then read-only: searching for receipts and invoices that are missing from the books.
  • Sign-in information: access tokens issued by Intuit and Google. We never receive or store anyone's QuickBooks or Google password.

3. How we use it

Only to keep the connected company's books: matching documents to transactions, proposing how an expense is coded, attaching documents to the right records, and producing reports. Nothing is written to QuickBooks without the company owner's approval.

4. Where it is kept and how it is protected

  • Data is stored on the company's own computer and backed up to the company's own Google Shared Drive.
  • Sign-in tokens are encrypted by the operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager) and are never written to files or source code.
  • The application's screens are reachable only on that computer, behind personal logins with roles and an idle time-out. Every change is recorded in a tamper-evident log.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone for their own purposes. Service providers process it only to run the application:

  • Intuit (QuickBooks Online) and Google (Drive and, where connected, Gmail), under their own terms;
  • Anthropic, PBC: copies of individual documents (and, at most, the names of accounts, vendors and items) may be sent to Anthropic's AI service to read a document or suggest its coding. Sign-in credentials are never sent.

We may disclose information where the law requires it.

6. Retention and deletion

Bookkeeping records are kept as long as Canadian tax rules require (generally six years after the year they relate to). The company owner can disconnect PSS Ledger from QuickBooks at any time (in QuickBooks: Apps, then Manage your apps), which revokes access, and can ask us to delete data that no longer has to be kept.

7. Your choices

To ask what information we hold, to correct it, or to have it deleted, write to brad@bepresentmedia.ca. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

8. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change, with a new effective date.
